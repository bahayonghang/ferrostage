## External primary references

- [Beamer maintained manual source, slide transitions](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/josephwright/beamer/main/doc/beamerug-animations.tex): transition belongs to the incoming page; overlay stepping, page dwell time, and media animation are distinct concepts.
- [Adobe PDF Reference 1.7, section 8.3.3, Table 8.13](https://opensource.adobe.com/dc-acrobat-sdk-docs/pdfstandards/pdfreference1.7old.pdf): Fade blends pages, Push moves old/new pages in the declared direction, Dissolve progressively reveals the incoming page. `/D` is transition duration; page `/Dur` is different.
- [Adobe PDTrans API](https://opensource.adobe.com/dc-acrobat-sdk-docs/acrobatsdk/apireference/PD_Layer/PDTrans.html): `/Trans` describes the effect and duration when entering a page.
- [PDFium render API](https://pdfium.googlesource.com/pdfium/+/e8c1d4144/public/fpdfview.h): the render call outputs a page bitmap. Combined with the local adapter, this supports implementing transition composition above rasterization rather than expecting a bitmap render to play a timeline.
